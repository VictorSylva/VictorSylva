### Cosmoo

**A food price-locking and food access infrastructure experiment.**

Cosmoo explores how people can prepay for food units at a defined
National Base Price and later redeem those units through participating
partner stores.

The system combines payments, food-unit accounting, partner-store
settlement logic and a digital food wallet.

**Areas I'm exploring:**
- Fintech infrastructure
- Food security
- Price-risk management
- Digital wallets
- Payment systems
- Partner-store settlement
- Data-driven pricing
- Firebase architecture

🔗 https://cosmoo.cc
